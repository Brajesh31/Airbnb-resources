# 🤝 Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The project welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and clear reasoning.
- Give actionable feedback about code and documentation.
- Respect privacy, licensing, and intellectual property.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberate disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer, Brajesh Kumar (`Brajesh31`), privately through GitHub with the relevant context. Reports will be reviewed as fairly and discreetly as possible.
